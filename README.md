# Harris Computer

Harris Computer is a constellation of companies acquiring and operating vertical market software businesses across the public sector, healthcare, utilities, financial services, education, and insurance markets. Harris Computer is a subsidiary of Constellation Software Inc. and operates dozens of portfolio companies that deliver mission-critical software to their respective verticals.

**APIs.json:** [apis.yml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/harris/refs/heads/main/apis.yml)

## APIs

Harris Computer itself does not publish a unified public developer portal. APIs and integrations are delivered through its individual portfolio companies, including Harris Local Government, Harris Utilities, Harris Healthcare (including Harris Ambulatory Care Enterprise), Harris School Solutions, and Harris Public Safety.
